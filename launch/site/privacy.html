⬢ HELIX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

The short version: the HELIX app stores your workspace on your device. We don't have a database of user content, we don't run ad trackers, and we can't read your businesses' data — the architecture makes it impossible, not just against policy.

What we collect

  • Nothing, by default. The app runs in your browser; workspaces, documents, sites and API keys stay in your browser's local storage on your device.
  • Opt-in signup beacon: if you tick "count my signup" when creating a HELIX ID, your name and email are sent to us once so we know you joined. Untick it and nothing is sent.
  • Payments: handled entirely by Stripe. We see what Stripe shows merchants (name, email, subscription status) — never your card number.
  • Hosting logs: our host (Netlify) keeps standard server logs (IP, request path) to serve and secure the site.

What we never collect

  • Your workspace content, documents, websites, chats, or Brain data
  • API keys you add to integrations (they stay in your local workspace)
  • Advertising identifiers — there are no third-party ad trackers on this site

Your controls

  • Export or delete all app data any time from inside the app (it's your device — clearing browser data removes everything).
  • Encrypted backups you create are locked with your passphrase; we can't read them.
  • To remove a signup-beacon record or ask anything else, reply to any email from us or your Stripe receipt.

Changes

Material changes will be posted here with a new date.
